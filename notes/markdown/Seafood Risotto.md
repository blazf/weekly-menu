Seafood Risotto  
  
Prep Time: 15 min  
Cook Time: 35 min  
Serves: 8 to 10 servings  
   
**Ingredients**  
* 1 tablespoon ++[olive oil](http://www.foodterms.com/encyclopedia/olive-oil/index.html)++  
* 1 cup chopped onions  
* Salt  
* Freshly ground ++[white pepper](http://www.foodterms.com/encyclopedia/white-pepper/index.html)++  
* Freshly ground black pepper  
* 6 cups seafood stock  
* 1 teaspoon chopped garlic  
* 1 pound (2 cups) ++[Arborio rice](http://www.foodterms.com/encyclopedia/arborio-rice/index.html)++  
* 2 pounds assorted Mediterranean shellfish and seafood  
* 1 tablespoon butter  
* 1/4 cup ++[heavy cream](http://www.foodterms.com/encyclopedia/cream/index.html)++  
* 1/2 cup freshly grated ++[Parmigiano-Reggiano](http://www.foodterms.com/encyclopedia/parmigiano-reggiano/index.html)++  
* 3 tablespoons chopped green onions, green part only  
* 2 tablespoons finely chopped fresh ++[parsley leaves](http://www.foodterms.com/encyclopedia/parsley/index.html)++  
   
**Directions**  
In a large saute pan, over medium heat, heat the oil. Add the onions. Season with salt and pepper, and cook, stirring. Saute until the onions are slightly soft, about 3 minutes. Add the stock and garlic. Bring the mixture to a boil, reduce the heat to medium, and simmer for about 6 minutes. Add the rice and simmer for 12 minutes, stirring constantly.  
Clean, trim and dice the shellfish and seafood. Season the seafood with salt and pepper. Continue to cook for 6 minutes. Stir in the butter, cream, cheese, and ++[green onions](http://www.foodterms.com/encyclopedia/green-onion/index.html)++. Simmer for 2 minutes, stirring constantly. Remove from the heat. Spoon the risotto in the center of each shallow bowl. Garnish with parsley.
