# Baked Tangerine Cheesecake (Serves 8–10)  
#   
**Ingredients**  
  
Base  
* 200 g digestive biscuits (or graham crackers)  
* 100 g butter, melted  
  
Filling  
* 600 g full-fat cream cheese, room temperature  
* 150 g caster (granulated) sugar  
* 3 large eggs, room temperature  
* 200 ml sour cream (or full-fat Greek yogurt)  
* Zest of 2 tangerines  
* 120 ml fresh tangerine juice (about 3–4 tangerines)  
* 1 tsp vanilla extract  
* 1 tbsp cornflour (cornstarch) or plain flour  
  
Optional topping  
* Tangerine segments or a simple tangerine glaze (recipe below)  
  
⸻  
  
**Method**  
	1.	Prep  
* Heat oven to 160°C (320°F).  
* Line the base of a 20 cm (8-inch) springform tin.  
	2.	Base  
* Crush biscuits and mix with melted butter.  
* Press firmly into the tin.  
* Bake for 10 minutes, then cool slightly.  
	3.	Filling  
* Beat cream cheese and sugar until smooth (don’t overbeat).  
* Beat in eggs one at a time.  
* Mix in sour cream, tangerine zest, juice, vanilla, and cornflour.  
	4.	Bake  
* Pour filling over base and smooth the top.  
* Bake for 50–60 minutes until edges are set but the centre still wobbles slightly.  
	5.	Cool gently (important!)  
* Turn oven off, crack the door open, and leave cheesecake inside for 1 hour.  
* Remove and cool completely, then chill for at least 4 hours (overnight best).  
  
⸻  
  
Optional Tangerine Glaze  
* 120 ml tangerine juice  
* 2 tbsp sugar  
* 1 tsp cornflour mixed with 1 tbsp water  
  
Simmer juice and sugar, whisk in cornflour slurry, cook until thickened. Cool before pouring over cheesecake.  
  
⸻  
  
Tips for Success  
* Room-temperature ingredients = smooth cheesecake.  
* Slight wobble in the centre is perfect — it firms up as it chills.  
* If your tangerines are very sweet, add 1–2 tsp lemon juice for balance.
