Rizev narastek  
  
Ingredients:  
* 300g rise  
* 1l milk  
* 60g butter  
* 3-4 eggs  
* Shredded lemon pill  
* Vanilla sugar  
* 4 spoons of sugar  
* Pinch of salt  
* Option: 100g of cottage chees  
* 2-3 apples (or pears, ananas, …)  
   
Procedure:  
* In boiling milk add rise and butter and cook 20 minutes and cool down  
* Create snow from white parts of eggs with half sugar (2 spoons)  
* Mix yellow parts of eggs with rest of sugar till foam, add vanilla sugar, lemon stuff  
* Mix in cooked rise  
* Mix in show with hand  
* Shred apples  
* Put in the pen: rise, then apples, then rise  
* 30 minutes at 180 degrees (till gold crust on top)
