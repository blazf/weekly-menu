**Lasagna**  
  
**Ingredients**  
	•	9 lasagna noodles  
	•	1 lb (450 g) ground beef  
	•	1 jar (24 oz / 680 g) pasta sauce (any tomato or marinara sauce)  
	•	2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese  
	•	1 ½ cups ricotta cheese (or cottage cheese)  
	•	½ cup grated Parmesan cheese  
	•	1 egg  
	•	1 tsp salt  
	•	½ tsp pepper  
	•	1 tsp Italian seasoning (optional)  
  
⸻  
  
**Instructions**  
	1.	**Preheat oven:**  
Preheat your oven to **375°F (190°C)**.  
	2.	**Cook noodles:**  
Boil lasagna noodles according to package directions. Drain and set aside.  
	3.	**Cook the meat:**  
In a large skillet, cook **ground beef** over medium heat until browned.  
Drain any fat. Add **pasta sauce**, stir, and simmer for about **5 minutes**.  
	4.	**Mix the cheese filling:**  
In a bowl, combine **ricotta, egg, Parmesan, salt, pepper**, and Italian seasoning.  
	5.	**Assemble the lasagna:**  
	•	Spread a thin layer of **meat sauce** on the bottom of a 9x13-inch baking dish.  
	•	Add **3 noodles**.  
	•	Spread **⅓ of the ricotta mixture** over noodles.  
	•	Add **¼ of the mozzarella** and **¼ of the sauce**.  
	•	Repeat layers twice more (noodles → ricotta → mozzarella → sauce).  
	•	Finish with noodles, sauce, and remaining mozzarella on top.  
	6.	**Bake:**  
Cover with foil and bake for **25 minutes**.  
Remove foil and bake another **15 minutes**, until cheese is melted and bubbly.  
	7.	**Rest and serve:**  
Let it rest **10–15 minutes** before cutting so it holds together nicely.
