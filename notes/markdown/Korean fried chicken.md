# Korean fried chicken  
  
**Ingredients**  
  
**For the Shrimp:**  
	•	1 lb (450g) large shrimp, peeled and deveined  
	•	Salt and pepper to taste  
	•	1 tsp garlic powder  
	•	1 tsp onion powder  
	•	½ cup cornstarch  
	•	½ cup all-purpose flour  
	•	1 egg  
	•	½ cup cold water (adjust as needed)  
	•	Oil for deep frying  
  
**For the Korean Sauce:**  
	•	2 tbsp gochujang (Korean red chili paste)  
	•	1 tbsp soy sauce  
	•	2 tbsp honey or corn syrup  
	•	1 tbsp brown sugar  
	•	1 tbsp rice vinegar  
	•	2 cloves garlic, minced  
	•	1 tsp sesame oil  
	•	1 tsp grated ginger (optional)  
	•	Toasted sesame seeds & chopped green onions (for garnish)  
  
⸻  
  
**Instructions**  
  
**1. Prep the Shrimp**  
	•	Pat shrimp dry, season with salt, pepper, garlic powder, and onion powder.  
	•	Let it rest for 5–10 minutes while you prepare the batter.  
  
**2. Make the Batter**  
	•	In a bowl, mix flour and cornstarch.  
	•	In another bowl, beat the egg and mix with cold water.  
	•	Combine wet and dry to make a thick, pancake-like batter.  
  
**3. Fry the Shrimp (Double Fry)**  
	•	Heat oil to 350°F (175°C).  
	•	Dip shrimp in batter and fry in batches for 2–3 minutes until lightly golden.  
	•	Remove and drain on a wire rack.  
	•	Increase oil temperature to 375°F (190°C), then re-fry for 1–2 minutes until ultra crispy.  
	•	Drain again.  
  
**4. Make the Sauce**  
	•	In a pan over medium heat, combine gochujang, soy sauce, honey, sugar, vinegar, garlic, sesame oil, and ginger.  
	•	Simmer for 2–3 minutes until slightly thickened.  
  
**5. Coat the Shrimp**  
	•	Toss hot, double-fried shrimp in the sauce just before serving.  
	•	Garnish with sesame seeds and green onions.  
  
⸻  
  
**Tips**  
	•	Don’t overcrowd the pan during frying to maintain oil temperature.  
	•	Serve immediately for max crispiness.  
	•	Great with pickled radish and cold beer, just like real Korean fried chicken.
