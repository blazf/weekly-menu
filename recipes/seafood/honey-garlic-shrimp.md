# Honey Garlic Shrimp

Quick, simple and big on flavour. Good over brown rice with steamed vegetables, or on a salad.

## Ingredients

- ⅓ cup honey
- ¼ cup soy sauce, reduced sodium
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp minced fresh ginger, optional
- 1 lb medium uncooked shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 2 tsp olive oil
- Chopped green onion, to garnish

## Method

1. Whisk the honey, soy sauce, garlic and ginger together in a medium bowl. **Use half as the marinade in step 2 and reserve half for cooking in step 4.**
2. Place the shrimp in a sealable container or zip-top bag. Pour half the mixture over, stir, and marinate in the refrigerator **15 minutes** — or up to 8–12 hours.
3. Refrigerate the reserved half separately.
4. Heat the olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Add the shrimp and discard the used marinade. Cook on one side until pink, about **45 seconds**, then flip. Pour in the reserved marinade and cook until the shrimp is cooked through, **1–2 minutes** more.
5. Serve with the cooked sauce and a garnish of green onion.

## Notes

- While the shrimp marinates, steam broccoli and microwave some quick brown rice — the whole meal lands together.
