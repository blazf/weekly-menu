# Sesame-Crusted Tuna Steak

Quick enough for a weeknight, good enough for company. If you like your tuna rare, buy it as fresh and as high quality as you can.

## Ingredients

- ½ cup soy sauce
- ¼ cup chopped scallions
- 2 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp ground fresh ginger
- 4 tuna steaks, about 6 oz each
- ½ cup sesame seeds, white and black combined
- ½ tsp cornstarch

## Method

1. Combine the soy sauce, scallions, lemon juice, sesame oil and ginger in a large zip-top bag. Add the tuna, turning to coat, and marinate in the refrigerator **20 minutes**.
2. Preheat a contact grill to its highest setting. Spread the sesame seeds on a plate.
3. Remove the tuna from the bag, brushing the scallions off and **reserving the marinade**. Coat each steak in sesame seeds on all sides, pressing so they stick.
4. Lightly spray the grill plates and place the steaks on the grill, closing the cover so the top plate rests evenly on them. Grill about **3 minutes**, longer if you prefer tuna cooked through. Remove and keep warm.
5. Pour the reserved marinade into a small saucepot and bring to a boil. Whisk in the cornstarch and cook **3–4 minutes** until thickened.
6. Drizzle the sauce over the tuna before serving.

## Notes

- Works on a grill pan or heavy skillet if you don't have a contact grill — sear about 90 seconds per side.
