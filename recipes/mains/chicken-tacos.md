# Chicken Tacos

> ℹ️ **Mostly reconstructed.** Only the fillings were given: chicken, avocado, sour
> cream, corn and tomatoes. Quantities, spices, tortillas, lime and the method are
> standard defaults, untested. Adjust freely.

Spiced seared chicken in warm tortillas with charred corn, tomato, avocado and sour cream.

## Ingredients

**For the chicken**
- 600 g boneless chicken thighs or breasts
- 1 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp salt

**For the toppings**
- 1 can (about 300 g) sweetcorn, drained
- 2 tomatoes, diced
- 2 ripe avocados, sliced or mashed
- 200 g sour cream
- 1 lime

**To serve**
- 8–12 small tortillas (corn or flour)

## Method

1. Toss the chicken with the oil, cumin, paprika, garlic powder and salt.
2. Sear in a hot pan over medium-high heat, **5–7 min per side**, until cooked through. Rest **5 min**, then slice thinly.
3. In the same pan, char the drained corn over high heat for **3–4 min**, stirring now and then.
4. Dice the tomatoes. Slice or mash the avocado and squeeze over half the lime so it doesn't brown.
5. Warm the tortillas in a dry pan, **about 30 s per side**.
6. Fill each tortilla with chicken, corn, tomato and avocado. Top with sour cream and a squeeze of lime.

## Notes

- Set everything out in bowls and let people build their own.
