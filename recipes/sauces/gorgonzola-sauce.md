# Gorgonzola Sauce

> ℹ️ **Whole recipe reconstructed.** No source recipe was provided. The quantities and steps follow the usual Italian approach and haven't been tested.

A rich, tangy blue-cheese cream sauce. Classic with gnocchi, penne or steak.

## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp butter
- 200 ml cream
- 120 g gorgonzola dolce, rind removed, cubed
- 2 tbsp grated Parmesan, optional
- Freshly cracked black pepper
- Pinch of grated nutmeg, optional
- Handful of chopped toasted walnuts, optional

## Method

1. Melt the butter in a small pan over **low–medium heat**. Add the cream and bring just to a gentle simmer.
2. Add the gorgonzola and stir until it has melted into a smooth sauce, **2–3 minutes**. Don't let it boil hard.
3. Stir in the Parmesan, if using, and simmer **1–2 minutes** more until it lightly coats a spoon.
4. Season with black pepper and nutmeg. It rarely needs salt — taste first.
5. Toss with the pasta or gnocchi straight away, loosening with a splash of cooking water if needed. Scatter with walnuts to serve.

## Notes

- Gorgonzola dolce melts smoother and is milder; piccante gives a sharper sauce — use a little less.
- The sauce thickens as it cools; add a splash of water or milk to bring it back.
