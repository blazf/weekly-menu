# Garlic Cream Sauce

> ℹ️ **Adapted.** The sauce from **Creamy Garlic Butter Shrimp Pasta** with the pasta and shrimp taken out. The original thinned it with starchy pasta water; the milk-or-water substitute is our own, untested.

A quick garlicky Parmesan cream sauce for pasta, gnocchi, chicken or seafood.

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp butter
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 3–4 cloves garlic, minced
- Pinch of red pepper flakes
- ¾ cup heavy cream
- ½ cup freshly grated Parmesan
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- Salt and freshly cracked black pepper
- 2–3 tbsp milk or water, to thin (or pasta water, if you're cooking pasta)
- 2 tbsp chopped fresh parsley, optional

## Method

1. Melt the butter with the olive oil in a wide skillet over **medium heat**. Add the garlic and red pepper flakes and sauté until fragrant, about **1 minute** — don't let the garlic brown.
2. Pour in the cream and simmer gently for **2 minutes** to reduce slightly. Turn the heat to **low**, stir in the Parmesan and whisk until smooth.
3. Stir in the lemon juice and a pinch of black pepper. Loosen with **2–3 tbsp** milk or water until glossy. Taste and add salt if needed.
4. Stir in the parsley and use straight away.

## Notes

- If you are serving it with pasta, use pasta water instead of milk, as the original recipe does — the starch helps the sauce cling.
- Lemon juice cuts through the richness of the cream; don't skip it.
