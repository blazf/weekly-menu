# Baked Potatoes

Classic oven-baked russets with crackly salted skin and a fluffy inside. Baking them without oil first and opening them as soon as they come out are what make them work.

## Ingredients

- 4 russet potatoes, medium to large
- 1–2 tbsp olive oil, or melted butter or bacon fat
- 1 tbsp coarse kosher salt, or sea salt

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to **425 °F (218 °C)** with a rack in the middle.
2. Scrub the potatoes under cold running water with a brush. Dry them completely with a clean towel — any moisture left on them stops the skin crisping.
3. Prick each potato **6–8 times** all over with a fork so steam can escape.
4. Put the potatoes directly on the oven rack, with a foil-lined baking sheet underneath for drips, or on a rimmed baking sheet. Bake them without oil or salt for **25 minutes** to dry out the skin.
5. Take them out, brush generously all over with oil and sprinkle with coarse salt so it sticks to the skin.
6. Bake another **20–30 minutes** (**45–55 minutes** in total, depending on size). They're done when the skin feels crackly and gives easily when squeezed with an oven mitt, or reads **205–210 °F (96–99 °C)** inside.
7. Open them straight away: cut a slit lengthwise along the top, then, protecting your hands with a towel, push the ends towards the centre so the potato pops open. Fluff the inside with a fork to let the steam out — left closed, the inside goes dense and gummy.

## Notes

- You'll need a baking sheet or an oven-safe wire rack.
- **Classic loaded:** butter, sour cream, sharp cheddar, crumbled crispy bacon and sliced chives or spring onions.
- **Texas style:** chopped leftover smoked brisket, warm barbecue sauce, pickled jalapeños and melted cheddar.
- **Simple:** good salted butter, black pepper and flaky sea salt.
