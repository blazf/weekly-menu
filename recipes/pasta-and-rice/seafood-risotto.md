# Seafood Risotto

## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 cup chopped onions
- Salt
- Freshly ground white pepper
- Freshly ground black pepper
- 6 cups seafood stock
- 1 tsp chopped garlic
- 1 lb (2 cups) Arborio rice
- 2 lb assorted Mediterranean shellfish and seafood
- 1 tbsp butter
- ¼ cup heavy cream
- ½ cup freshly grated Parmigiano-Reggiano
- 3 tbsp chopped green onions, green part only
- 2 tbsp finely chopped fresh parsley

## Method

1. Heat the oil in a large saute pan over medium heat. Add the onions, season with salt and pepper, and cook, stirring, until slightly soft — about **3 minutes**.
2. Add the stock and garlic. Bring to a boil, reduce the heat to medium and simmer about **6 minutes**.
3. Add the rice and simmer **12 minutes**, stirring constantly.
4. Clean, trim and dice the shellfish and seafood, and season with salt and pepper. Add to the pan and cook **6 minutes**.
5. Stir in the butter, cream, cheese and green onions. Simmer **2 minutes**, stirring constantly, then remove from the heat.
6. Spoon into shallow bowls and garnish with parsley.
