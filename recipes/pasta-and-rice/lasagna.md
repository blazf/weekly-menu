# Lasagna

Classic layered beef and three-cheese lasagna.

## Ingredients

- 9 lasagna noodles
- 1 lb (450 g) ground beef
- 1 jar (24 oz / 680 g) pasta sauce, tomato or marinara
- 2 cups shredded mozzarella
- 1½ cups ricotta, or cottage cheese
- ½ cup grated Parmesan
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp salt
- ½ tsp pepper
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning, optional

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to **375°F (190°C)**.
2. Boil the noodles according to package directions. Drain and set aside.
3. Brown the ground beef in a large skillet over medium heat. Drain the fat, add the pasta sauce, stir and simmer **5 minutes**.
4. Combine the ricotta, egg, Parmesan, salt, pepper and Italian seasoning in a bowl.
5. Assemble in a 9x13-inch dish: a thin layer of meat sauce on the bottom, then 3 noodles, ⅓ of the ricotta, ¼ of the mozzarella, ¼ of the sauce. Repeat twice more, then finish with noodles, sauce and the remaining mozzarella.
6. Cover with foil and bake **25 minutes**. Uncover and bake another **15 minutes**, until melted and bubbly.
7. Rest **10–15 minutes** before cutting so it holds together.
