# Chicken Alfredo

> ℹ️ **One quantity estimated.** The source lists ricotta with no amount. 1 cup
> balances the pint of cream and cup of Parmesan; adjust so the sauce coats the pasta
> rather than clumping.

A rich Alfredo sauce of butter, ricotta, cream and Parmesan, served with fettuccini and sauteed chicken.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb fettuccini
- 1½ cups butter, divided
- 1 lb skinless boneless chicken breast, cut into cubes
- 2 (16 oz) containers whole milk
- 1 cup ricotta cheese *(estimated — see note above)*
- 1 pint heavy cream
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 cup grated Parmesan

## Method

1. Bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a boil. Add the fettuccini and cook **8–10 minutes** until al dente, then drain.
2. Melt 2 tbsp butter in a large skillet over medium heat. Saute the chicken until no longer pink and the juices run clear.
3. In a large saucepan combine the ricotta, cream, salt, Parmesan and remaining butter. Cook over medium heat until well combined, about **10 minutes**.
4. Stir in the cooked fettuccini and chicken, and cook until heated through.
