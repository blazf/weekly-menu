# Mushroom Risotto

## Ingredients

- 6 cups chicken broth
- 3 tbsp olive oil, divided
- 1 lb portobello mushrooms, thinly sliced
- 1 lb white mushrooms, thinly sliced
- 2 shallots, diced
- 1½ cups Arborio rice
- ½ cup dry white wine
- Sea salt
- Freshly ground black pepper
- 3 tbsp finely chopped chives
- 4 tbsp butter
- ⅓ cup freshly grated Parmesan

## Method

1. Warm the broth in a saucepan over low heat and keep it there.
2. Warm 2 tbsp olive oil in a large saucepan over medium-high heat. Stir in the mushrooms and cook until soft, about **3 minutes**. Remove the mushrooms and their liquid and set aside.
3. Add 1 tbsp olive oil to the pan and stir in the shallots. Cook **1 minute**. Add the rice, stirring to coat with oil, about **2 minutes**.
4. When the rice has taken on a pale golden color, pour in the wine and stir constantly until fully absorbed.
5. Add ½ cup broth and stir until absorbed. Continue adding broth ½ cup at a time, stirring continuously, until the liquid is absorbed and the rice is al dente — **15 to 20 minutes**.
6. Remove from the heat and stir in the mushrooms with their liquid, the butter, chives and Parmesan. Season with salt and pepper.
