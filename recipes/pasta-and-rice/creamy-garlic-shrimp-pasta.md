# Creamy Garlic Butter Shrimp Pasta

A quick weeknight pasta for leftover cooked shrimp (e.g. Costco shrimp cocktail). The shrimp only warm through at the end so they stay tender.

## Ingredients

- Remaining cooked shrimp, tails removed (chop into bite-sized pieces if you only have a few)
- 8 oz (225 g) pasta — fettuccine, linguine or penne
- 2 tbsp butter
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 3–4 cloves garlic, minced
- ¾ cup heavy cream
- ½ cup freshly grated Parmesan
- ½ cup reserved pasta water
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- Salt, freshly cracked black pepper, pinch of red pepper flakes
- 2 tbsp chopped fresh parsley, optional

## Method

1. Cook the pasta in well-salted boiling water until al dente, **1 minute less than the package says**. Scoop out ½ cup of the pasta water before draining.
2. While the pasta cooks, melt the butter with the olive oil in a wide skillet over **medium heat**. Add the garlic and red pepper flakes and sauté until fragrant, about **1 minute** — don't let the garlic brown.
3. Pour in the cream and simmer gently for **2 minutes** to reduce slightly. Turn the heat to **low**, stir in the Parmesan and whisk until smooth.
4. Stir in the lemon juice, a pinch of black pepper and **2–3 tbsp** of the pasta water for a glossy sauce. Taste and add salt if needed.
5. Add the drained pasta and the shrimp to the skillet. Toss continuously on low heat for **60–90 seconds**, just until the shrimp are warm and the pasta is coated. Splash in more pasta water if the sauce gets too thick.
6. Garnish with parsley and extra black pepper or Parmesan. Serve immediately.

## Notes

- The shrimp are already cooked — don't recook them or they turn rubbery.
- Cooked shrimp keep **3–4 days** in the fridge (at or below 4 °C). Check they smell neutral and aren't slimy before using.
- Lemon juice cuts through the richness of the cream; don't skip it.
