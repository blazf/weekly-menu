# Baked Tangerine Cheesecake

Citrus baked cheesecake on a digestive biscuit base.

## Ingredients

**For the base**
- 200 g digestive biscuits, or graham crackers
- 100 g butter, melted

**For the filling**
- 600 g full-fat cream cheese, room temperature
- 150 g caster sugar
- 3 large eggs, room temperature
- 200 ml sour cream, or full-fat Greek yogurt
- Zest of 2 tangerines
- 120 ml fresh tangerine juice, about 3–4 tangerines
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 tbsp cornflour, or plain flour

**For the optional glaze**
- 120 ml tangerine juice
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp cornflour mixed with 1 tbsp water

## Method

1. Heat the oven to **160°C (320°F)** and line the base of a 20 cm springform tin.
2. Crush the biscuits, mix with the melted butter and press firmly into the tin. Bake **10 minutes**, then cool slightly.
3. Beat the cream cheese and sugar until smooth — don't overbeat. Beat in the eggs one at a time.
4. Mix in the sour cream, tangerine zest, juice, vanilla and cornflour.
5. Pour the filling over the base and smooth the top. Bake **50–60 minutes**, until the edges are set but the centre still wobbles slightly.
6. Turn the oven off, crack the door open and leave the cheesecake inside **1 hour**.
7. Remove and cool completely, then chill at least **4 hours** — overnight is best.
8. For the glaze, simmer the juice and sugar, whisk in the cornflour slurry and cook until thickened. Cool before pouring over the cheesecake.

## Notes

- Room-temperature ingredients give a smooth cheesecake.
- A slight wobble in the centre is correct — it firms up as it chills.
- If the tangerines are very sweet, add 1–2 tsp lemon juice for balance.
- Top with tangerine segments instead of glaze if you prefer.
