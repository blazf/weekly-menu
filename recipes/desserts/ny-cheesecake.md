# New York Cheesecake

Dense baked cheesecake on a graham cracker base. The long cooling is what keeps the top from cracking, so start early.

## Ingredients

**For the base**
- 15 graham crackers, crushed
- 30 g butter, melted

**For the filling**
- 896 g cream cheese
- 300 g white sugar
- 180 ml milk
- 4 eggs
- 230 g sour cream
- 15 ml vanilla extract
- 30 g all-purpose flour

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to **350°F (175°C)**. Grease a 9-inch springform pan.
2. Mix the graham cracker crumbs with the melted butter and press onto the bottom of the pan.
3. Mix the cream cheese with the sugar until smooth. Blend in the milk, then mix in the eggs one at a time, mixing just enough to incorporate.
4. Mix in the sour cream, vanilla and flour until smooth. Pour the filling into the prepared crust.
5. Bake **1 hour**.
6. **Turn the oven off and leave the cake inside with the door closed for 5 to 6 hours.** This is what prevents cracking.
7. Chill in the refrigerator until serving.
