# Wafer-Thin Waffles

Thin and crisp. For the thick fluffy kind see Belgian Waffles.

## Ingredients

- 300 g flour
- 1 sachet baking powder
- 1 pinch salt
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 2 eggs
- 400 ml milk

## Method

1. Beat the eggs to a smooth consistency.
2. Add the remaining ingredients and beat together into a smooth batter.
3. Cook in a hot waffle iron, **about 6–8 minutes** per batch, until crisp.
