# American Pancakes

Thick, fluffy pancakes. For thin crepes see Palačinke.

## Ingredients

- 135 g plain flour
- 1 tsp baking powder
- ½ tsp salt
- 2 tbsp caster sugar
- 130 ml milk
- 1 large egg, lightly beaten
- 2 tbsp melted butter, cooled slightly, plus extra for cooking
- Maple syrup and butter, to serve

## Method

1. Sift the flour, baking powder, salt and sugar into a large bowl.
2. In a separate bowl, lightly whisk the milk and egg together, then whisk in the melted butter.
3. Pour the milk mixture into the flour mixture and beat with a fork until smooth. Any lumps will disappear with a little mixing. Let the batter stand a few minutes.
4. Heat a non-stick frying pan over medium heat and add a knob of butter. When melted, add a ladle of batter. It will seem very thick — that's correct.
5. Wait until the top begins to bubble, then turn and cook until both sides are golden brown and the pancake has risen to about **1 cm** thick.
6. Repeat until the batter is used up. They keep warm in a low oven but taste best straight from the pan.
7. Serve with maple syrup and extra butter.
